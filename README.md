# NithyaSeva

A digital worker-service marketplace designed to connect workers with job opportunities and support transparent transactions and worker empowerment.

## Problem Statement

Workers can face difficulties finding reliable job opportunities, accessing transparent payment records, and building trust with customers. NithyaSeva aims to address these challenges through a digital marketplace.

## Objectives

- Connect workers with people seeking services.
- Allow customers to post jobs and workers to find suitable opportunities.
- Support transparent job and payment records.
- Build trust through ratings and reviews.
- Empower workers through individual profiles and welfare-oriented features.

## Planned Features

- User registration and login
- Worker profiles and service listings
- Job posting and matching
- Job acceptance and completion tracking
- Payment records and transaction history
- Ratings and reviews
- Role-based access for customers and workers
